Online Consulting

Can't travel to Morva? Book a video consultation and connect with your physician on WhatsApp.

How Online Consulting Works

From booking to prescription, in four steps

Step 01

Choose a physician

Browse specialities and pick the doctor who fits your concern.

Step 02

Pick a slot & consent

Select a free time slot and confirm consent for a video consultation.

Step 03

Get confirmed by email

You and the physician both receive the appointment, with a WhatsApp and Zoom link.

Step 04

Consult by video / WhatsApp

Join the video call at your slot; message the physician directly on WhatsApp for follow-up.

Start a Booking
The College

Teaching homoeopathy since 2017

Founded under Shri Panchmahal Jilla Pachhat Varg Kelvani Mandal, the college offers a Direct BHMS programme with a 100-seat annual intake, recognised by the National Commission for Homoeopathy and affiliated with Shree Govind Guru University. A 2023 MoU with CCRH links our teaching to national research.
